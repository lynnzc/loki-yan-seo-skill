# Dimension: rejections (English)

Source: tweets only — `/workspace/loki-yan/corpus.md` and `corpus.json` (**978** posts). No blog, no decks, no imported SEO frameworks.

Evidence is **@loki_yan_seo authored only** (532 of 978). Other people's replies in the dump are ignored.

Two rejection types used throughout:

- **metric-as-causal** — he still looks at the number (or the tool), but refuses it as the *reason* something ranks / converts / should be a KPI.
- **entire-practice** — he wants the move itself stopped, or treats the concept as fake / a trap / logically invalid.

If he still opens the thing as a **reporting / research panel**, that is noted on the same row.
