## §1 Core philosophy — what SEO is / is not

### 1.1 SEO does three things. Writing blogs is not SEO.

Traditional Search “does three things: crawl sites, index sites, serve (ranking)” (id=2091364706703581191). He nails the boundary in both languages, weeks apart: “写博客不叫做SEO。” (id=2092059738062348681) “Writing blog posts != SEO.” (id=2081519248011370590)

On a serious long-term project, “SEO 铺词” as a concept does not exist (id=2090954259680694666). “铺词，铺内容这两个东西本质就是伪命题。” (id=2089165558516437461) Named companies and agencies “都没有花钱招人写博客和做外链”; the writing seat is a separate Content / Editor / Content Strategist hire (id=2091060807065117104). All-blog sites still have to answer “你们有转化么？我觉得应该做money page。” (id=2091775475635073373)

Doing SEO (and future GEO) is “catch 需求”: one plate of monthly searches, everyone fighting for the same demand (id=2091668405367791619). The point is not charity: “做SEO是为了赚钱，不是为了为爱发电。” What you optimize is “your Money Page, your Trust.” High-ticket buyers “不会轻易因为一个Reddit或者一篇ChatGPT写的博客就成为你的付费用户。” (id=2090983234146574708) “转化和兜里的钱才有价值。流量是为了变现，不变现做流量就是为爱发电。” (id=2033807753547043235)

SEO is glue / foundation: “the process of maximizing a site and a brand's exposure on search engines.” Later, under AI, it is still maximizing site and brand exposure in each place (id=2091463375003983897). He splits two kinds of SEO `[once]`: growth (rank, traffic, conversion) vs Maintain leadership at Canva / Zillow / Booking — “阻止别人瞎搞” — “打江山和守江山的区别。” (id=2091321150127366357)

### 1.2 Quality is a site-wide ranking factor. Its opposite is Spam. EEAT itself is not.

Earliest law in the dump: “EEAT is not a ranking factor (Google said). EEAT determines quality, and Google said Quality is a ranking factor.” (id=1978391845844426780)

He restates the Shanghai official PPT: Google first called Quality a ranking factor in 2025; “Quality应该考虑网站的整体。” (id=2092027807719444899) “Quality 应该是site-wide的singal.” “Quality的对立面就是Spam.” “你的Quality不如竞争对手高，你就排不过竞争对手。” (id=2091353930425127142)

Quality’s definition is two words: “Quality 的定义是Effort 和Original” (id=2091362717907251353). Content that can rank: close to human-written + has Quality + Quality = Effort + Original. “这里的原创可不是你找几个网站去缝合。” (id=2091362717907251353)

March tension, keep both `[once]`: “It's not about quality. It's about how it was created. If you make your own input there, it is more likely to rank.” (id=2033808549445570669) Same human-input insistence; the English word “quality” flips. Do not quote one without the other.

Spam as an operating definition: “研究了一个AI Agent，从各个网站拿别人的东西过来缝合洗稿。” (id=2091353930425127142) “网站权重并不能抵消掉 AI Spam, AI Slope, 垃圾博客带来的负面影响。” (id=2089165558516437461) His reconstructed ledger `[once, reconstructed]`: “For each spam found on your website, spam score +1.” Things that “肯定被干” are Clocking (his spelling of cloaking), AI draft-washing collage, AI flooding, Link Spam (id=2090396597066899635). “It hurts your ranking in long term, most of the AIGC content is lack of quality.” (id=2033533787267899583)

Quality can be UX, site content, Core Web Vitals, “all the details mentioned in QRG”; it can also be “所有产品照片都是自己拿单反实拍” and every alt / product description written by hand (id=2091353930425127142). Original photos “对于网站的‘质量’加成很高” `[asserted]` (id=2088803453565059194).

### 1.3 The EEAT door is Effort + Originality. Generic automation cannot cross it. Tokens are not Effort.

“我一直说我不信纯SEO Automation的原因就是Google 设置了EEAT这个门槛。” “这里有2个关键点：Effort和Originality。” “通用型SEO Automation 怎么跨过Effort和Originality? By 花了多少Token ?” (id=2092095823731462302) “不是不要做automation.” His own process / agent / prompts are fine. “但是你要做一个通用的Agent卖，我觉得你得要再想想。” (id=2092073561171398727) The question is “技术上实现不了，还是逻辑上不通？” (id=2092067172021608918) Semi-auto beats full-auto, especially on Google (id=2033806758175772940).

The no-error floor: “你做任何批量的事情，至少要有增量…不然一定会被判定为low  effort，然后被干。” `[overclaim on 一定]` Original text, original images, original video, original insights can all be counted as contentEffort (id=2092090944648581505). “自动化，AIGC的东西可能还没有办法做到，没有人为干预的情况下，完全实现 Non-Commodity的这个概念。” (id=2091487107806323136) “用AI批量生成博客会帮助到网站和生意” fails “从Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning 数学每一个角度” `[overclaim]` (id=2070420648900403202).

Warehouse field: `contentEffort - LLM-based effort estimation for article pages` (id=2092090944648581505). Google Cloud has an AI Image Detection API: even if it will not punish you, “你contentEffort分数一定不会是 正 的.” `[overclaim / inference]` Hire a sharp intern + Canva and make images by hand (id=2092100375239438810).

“SEO的不可能三角，即Scale，Quality，Speed。你不太可能在不花钱的情况下同时实现上述三件事情。” `[once]` (id=2090567084933787767)

PSEO without an original spine (your own algorithm, rating system, data, or product) “不然半年内就会被砸掉. 无论网站权重多高，只要PSEO和铺的内容不是原创的，基本上今年都要出问题。” (id=2088639745807278589)

### 1.4 The asset is always brand and people. Content is the hardest barrier. Fake authors are a red line.

“为什么我一直说做SEO最难的是内容” (id=2091487107806323136) “内容是SEO的门槛。高水平写手是企业资产。” “SEO的资产永远是品牌和人，你细品。” Wirecutter keeps 150+ named editors rather than an AI agent (id=2090735573443158517). “所有内容都是手搓，不存在任何Agent，Workflow。” “SEO就那样，没有捷径，护城河要靠人来堆” `[asserted, his news site]` (id=2064883047825395749) “我们做SEO的离不开 Content Writer” (id=2090289091011391554) “谷歌设置了EEAT以后，护城河就是人本身。” (id=2091840249198793094)

He pays senior copy AU$800–1000 / long landing, 300–500 / blog, 200 / product page — a market price for effort, not an industry rate card (id=2071839385998569488). “SEO建站能赚钱的核心不是vibe coding，而是产品和内容” (id=2071775046205612299).

Fake authors are the floor: “假作者肯定被谷歌枪毙，这玩意是底线。” `[overclaim on 肯定]` Fallback if you still batch: `"@type":"Organization"` (id=2091739865121783923). “写博客，不谈作者这个概念，就像谈恋爱不结婚一样，耍流氓。” QRG 4.5.3 on fake owner / creator profiles (id=2091381622616797394). Google looks at “作者实体、公开履历、领英账号、业背景，以及这些信息是否能被交叉验证。” (id=2033390752001335397) “如果你没办法验证真实性的话，不如不要放作者。或者就放你网站自己了。” (id=2091744284668305427) “作者连你 才行，不然没用。” — a one-way author box is dead (id=2091755868954231087). “换成新的 很有可能你的排名一下子就会上去” is a one-tweet recovery promise — treat as `[overclaim]` (id=2033817449922245047).

The “hardest / preferred” EEAT move: LinkedIn ↔ site entity linking. “提高SEO的最硬的一个方式，其实是linkedin。” (id=2090750446839476639) “Educational Degrees, Employment History 这俩东西，整个地球上只有 Linkedin 可以做到” `[once, overclaim]`. So the author’s LinkedIn links to the site, the site links to the author’s LinkedIn — that is the 优解. Xiaohongshu / WeChat “应该没用” as Google author authority (id=2091381622616797394). Convenient path: site A ↔ developer site ↔ GitHub/LinkedIn (id=2091198495197913207). If you will not show LinkedIn, “搞一个github，就是厉害一点的github，activity 高一点的.” (id=2092098493695553818)

### 1.5 Vertical, small-radius, stay focus. DA cannot cover off-topic.

“我的意思是垂直主题的网站，会更成功” (id=2091895491982405998) “小半径(siteRadius）的网站在垂直领域更容易成功” “千万不要东做一点，西做一点，乱铺内容，要stay focus.” (id=2091894056687431704) “Site Focus基本上决定了你能排什么，你不能排什么.” Even if “authority再高，（DA, 外链再高），我也没有办法在这个网站上去排和旅游相关的内容” (id=2090291222556578070). Homepage set to SEO Consultant → “我喜欢唱跳Rap，我能放下我的网站上去做排名吗？当然不行” (id=2091185632202883243). Homepage + About definition must cover the topics you want; too wide = slow, too narrow = you will have to change (id=2091897363598315734). Warehouse names (siteFocusScore / siteRadius) appear once; the *law* (stay vertical) is repeated.

About Us and Contact Us are mandatory. About must *qualify* the brand; that qualification affects what you can rank (id=2090291222556578070). About is “one of the most important pages for EEAT” and is “often treated like an afterthought.” Four jobs: who is behind it; what the business actually does; experience/credentials; whether content aligns with the real-world entity. “credibility…is built through site-wide consistency.” (id=2033192558889963680)

### 1.6 Backlinks / DA are useful. They are a result, not a cause. Do not chase them.

“突然意识到好像DA他不是排名的原因，而是结果。” Fixing Angular Soft 404, not buying DA, made traffic jump `[asserted]` (id=2091301352463102268). “外链到底有没有用？有用，但是不用追。追了会浪费钱。” Stopped a 20K USD/month budget — “后来停了没有半毛钱影响。” `[asserted]` (id=2090685656079241520) “One of the most persistent SEO myths is that Domain Authority and backlink volume are the most important ranking indicators.” Third-party authority metrics are “at best, rough proxies.” (id=2033189225483677758) “DA和外链参考为主。不用刻意去追求。” DA as a “how expensive is this vertical” proxy (id=2088636106271256974). “因为我以前做过 40 打90的，所以我觉得DR仅供参考就可以了。” `[asserted]` (id=2088606575581856225) “you don’t need domain rating to rank. Domain rating is old fashion now.” (id=2033717808757149896) He has seen ranks rise after deleting all Anchor Guest Posts (id=2090396597066899635).

Do not flatten this with the English reply that “Great product and websites gain backlinks organically. Google is based on links.” (id=2091305639599612383) — that is *earned* links as a byproduct, not a chase-links program.

YMYL / anything that touches money: the threshold is “权威性、专业性、合规性,” not backlinks. “搜索排名不是单纯的流量分发，而是信任分发。” (id=2033390752001335397) “EEAT里，特别是Trust 出问题的网站是致命的。” (id=2090697074153283687) Many topic sites have a threshold mechanism “（这个不是沙盒）.” “根据我的观察，YMYL的门槛应该是和域名绑定的。” `[once, 应该是]` (id=2091011325438955723) “crypto排名本质是合规，和SEO没太大关系。” `[once]` (id=2091741166094213429)

### 1.8 Non-English brands are structurally weaker in English-heavy LLMs. AI re-amplified information cocoons.

Observation only. No procedure. He framed it as “我一直在想一个问题”, clustered in March 2026, never restated as a closed law, never turned into “therefore write English pages / buy EN citations.”

### 1.9 Google never serves SEO people. The method is hypothesis → test → measure → iterate. Do not execute SOP mechanically.

He would hire for “有自己的判断能力，具有商业敏感度，不要机械的执行SOP。” (id=2091761728241766791) — §2 / §7 below are a scan order, not a list you can run with your brain off.

After you cross an Authority threshold, “比的就是页面对页面，微观层面了.” Then entity depth + social fame (id=2088627560033009736). ## §2 Operating engine — only steps he actually gave

If the tweets stop mid-method, this section stops. Missing steps live in §6. No imported audit checklists.

### 2.1 Everyday work: hypothesis, test, measure, iterate

**When:** The algorithm is a black box. “我们平时工作仍旧属于摸石头过河” (id=2091844216980979877)

**Steps:** Do not believe SEO magic → hypothesize → test → measure → iterate. He did not write how to file a hypothesis, how long to test, or what sheet to measure on. Those four words *are* the method.

### 2.2 Rankings drop in a big way — robots.txt first, Crawl status second

**When:** 网站出现排名大面积下滑. Used on 「知乎一夜之间丢掉谷歌流量」 (id=2091795112745689273)

1. First look at `robots.txt`.
2. Second look at Crawl status.
3. His diagnostic move: Chinese Google SERPs that “永远都是知乎” suddenly had no Zhihu → SEMRush → `robots.txt` had a problem.
4. Watch point: after `robots.txt` blocks all crawlers, watch traffic and index trend.

Audit item 3 is not written. Whether the harm was fatal “就不得而知了”.

### 2.3 contentEffort — the question to Gary, the engineer list, the no-error method

**When:** Unpacking the Warehouse leak; answering “提高 contentEffort 能提高 SEO，那我应该做什么” (id=2092090944648581505) (id=2092095823731462302) (id=2088131678065852712)

He asked Gary in Shanghai: about EEAT / QRG, does Google have an algorithm that runs automatically? How would effort be measured automatically? Google did not answer straight. The gist he kept: there is a “mathematic calculation” for the “things” on the EEAT Guideline. Then Warehouse: `contentEffort - LLM-based effort estimation for article pages`.

**Assume you are a Google Engineer (he says he is not; this is a hypothesis list, not a production script):**

1. Generic Template / generic structure written by AI?
2. Paraphrasing and stitching of the top 20 or top 10?
3. Unique entities, parameters? (he thinks ML can predict this)
4. Asset effort: are images and videos original / AI-generated? “我很确定谷歌是能分辨得出” `[overclaim]`
5. Information Gain: has what you wrote appeared before?

**What he tells operators, not the code above:** original text / images / video / insights = contentEffort. Any batch work needs increment, at least from data and content. Else low effort, then you get hit. Token spend is not Effort. No scoring formula, no tool, no threshold.

### 2.4 Buy human effort — intern + Canva, then ask ex-Google friends

**When:** After the Effort / Original talk. Small company, small studio, solo export (id=2092100375239438810)

1. Associate and hypothesize. Example: Cloud AI Image Detection exists; even if not punished, contentEffort “一定不会是 正 的.”
2. Hire a sharp domestic intern who learns fast — they want work experience, you want “human” effort.
3. Get a Canva account, make images by hand; you can do a lot in a month.
4. US friends: ask around what Google / ex-Google people have built; maybe it wires together.
5. “SEO要学Best Practice的目的，是在合理的范围内利用规则。”

No hiring brief, no Canva SOP, no “how to use the Cloud API.”

### 2.5 AI-product site: name the model, write the changelog, make download pages, or get a real GitHub

**When:** AI export sites. Friends who are ex-big-tech, PhD, CS (id=2092098493695553818)

1. Give the model a name. Ship a page / page set that walks versions and updates from day one — changelog-style, like App Store release notes. You already write engineer docs. Clarify and tell the story.
2. If you have an app: `/download`, `/download/ios`, `/download/android`, then link the stores.
3. Will not link LinkedIn? Get a serious GitHub with real activity.
4. “啥都不想努力，纯捞？那我没办法了.”

No page template, no schema, no “how much GitHub activity is enough.”

### 2.6 When you pick keywords, look at how many troops the other side has

**When:** Treat SEO as a war. Looking at KW Difficulty (id=2091668405367791619)

1. KD is opponent ammo / equipment / headcount, not how easy or hard the word is.

## §7 SOP for analyzing any site / task

### 7.1 Business lock (every task)

1. Is there an idea that can make money? Is the conversion path open? What you optimize is Money Page and Trust, not a Reddit thread or a ChatGPT blog (id=2090983234146574708).
2. Ordinary Digital Marketing not running → do not do GEO first (id=2064910376060498033).
3. Is this growth or hold-the-hill? Hold-the-hill’s first job is stopping other people from breaking traffic (id=2091321150127366357).

If this gate fails, later keywords and content are still charity.

### 7.2 Domain and qualification

4. New / expired domain: Semrush / Ahrefs / Majestic for links, Semrush / Ahrefs / SimilarWeb for traffic, Wayback for past life. Spam / PBN / 黄赌毒 history = hell-level start (id=2064981657497813013).
5. Must have About Us and Contact Us. About 定性 the brand — who / what / credentials / alignment with the real-world entity (id=2090291222556578070) (id=2033192558889963680).
6. Homepage query should be a `{modifier} + Entity` that can cover the next 3–5 years. Queries unrelated to that Entity do not belong on this site (id=2091185632202883243). Stay focus. East-and-west publishing blows out siteRadius and drops Focus (id=2091894056687431704).

### 7.3 Technical foundation

Technical SEO “是所有的Foundation” (id=2091301352463102268). On a collapse, this block moves to the front.

7. `robots.txt` — did someone ban the crawlers (id=2091795112745689273).
8. Crawl status (id=2091795112745689273).
9. Error pages must return HTTP 404, not 200. Soft 404 is a landmine (id=2091349098553295075).
10. `Raw = Rendered`, `Desktop = Mobile`, one HTML once, no `display:none`; no separate m-site, Canonical does not save it (id=2091508567119642847) (id=2091008050262110227). Screaming Frog paid for the render diff.
10b. JS-heavy: Chrome JS off, compare to apple.com/iphone, prefer CSS effects, core content must survive (id=2092105285955473656).
11. Mobile first screen has content; `<h1>` is on the first screen; cookie/ad overlays do not cover the fold. Test: GSC / Rich Results Test (id=2090686787534598639).
12. Semantic HTML: MC lives in `<main>`, do not Div to the bottom (id=2090738619808088255).
13. Login page present: scan QRG 8.4 — disclosure / firewall / register-recover / thin third-party login (id=2091376367740789153).
14. Before URL / domain / rebrand: URL versions controlled, 301s one-to-one, SEO Review first (id=2091309003397902703).

After tech is level, rank is the page’s final form: every tag, word, alt, anchor vs the competitor’s final form (id=2091301352463102268).

### 7.4 Author entity

15. Is each content page’s author a cross-checkable real person (resume, LinkedIn, background) (id=2033390752001335397).
16. 优解: author LinkedIn ↔ site (plus GitHub / developer site if technical) (id=2091381622616797394) (id=2091198495197913207). Author must link back (id=2091755868954231087).
17. Stop fake authors. If you must batch: Organization author, do not invent a person; if you got hit, delete (id=2091739865121783923). If you cannot verify, put no author, or put the site (id=2091744284668305427).

### 7.5 Keyword pick and page job

18. Every page: what is this page for, what does it rank, is the query unique and searched (id=2090954259680694666) (id=2090938323284185332). 铺词 does not exist.
19. Tools: Semrush / Ahrefs → Dataforseo → Keyword Planner (most accurate) → Trends. Split Trends by region; confirm users would actually search that way (id=2090954259680694666) (id=2091304086608236745).
20. When you look at KD, count the opponent’s SEO and content headcount on LinkedIn / careers pages. Do not send a fireteam at a battalion (id=2091668405367791619).
21. Query you cannot open: pair competitor `<title>` tags on the SERP. Check duplicated titles, core entity missing, default title, verb/modifier match (id=2091682678261375106).
22. Do not use KW Density (id=2091185632202883243). Do not treat DA as a cause (id=2091301352463102268).

### 7.6 contentEffort and site-wide Quality

23. Does the batch have information increment? Original text / images / video / insights? Increment-free batch = low effort (id=2092090944648581505).
24. Original ≠ stitching the top 10 (id=2091362717907251353). Token count ≠ Effort (id=2092095823731462302).
25. Quality is site-wide, not per-article. Spam on the site (stitching Agent, AI flood, soft-404 junk, Link Spam) taxes the whole site (id=2091353930425127142) (id=2090396597066899635).

Recovery is subtraction: disavow custom anchors, replace fake authors, 404 the junk, then reconsideration (id=2033817449922245047) (id=2037884887307526631). Do not promise “rank will jump.”
