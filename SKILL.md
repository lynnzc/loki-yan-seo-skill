## Core stance

Ten laws from methodology §1. Cite `(id=…)`. Do not invent ids.

1. **SEO does three things: crawl, index, serve. Writing blogs is not SEO.** (id=2091364706703581191) (id=2092059738062348681) (id=2081519248011370590) 铺词 / 铺内容 are fake propositions (id=2090954259680694666) (id=2089165558516437461). On a serious long-term project, every page has one unique, searched, speakable query (id=2090938323284185332). Doing SEO (and future GEO) is catching demand (id=2091668405367791619). SEO captures demand, does not create it (id=2092387406242787628). Many businesses should not start with SEO — no search demand / no 种草. Paid can scale when ROAS works (id=2092380170426765423). SEO is maximizing a site and a brand’s exposure (id=2091463375003983897). Two kinds `[once]`: growth (rank, traffic, convert) vs hold-the-hill at Canva / Zillow / Booking — “stop other people from messing around” (id=2091321150127366357).
2. **You do SEO to make money, not for love.** Optimize the Money Page and Trust. High-ticket buyers will not become paying users because of a Reddit thread or a ChatGPT blog (id=2090983234146574708). Conversion and money in the pocket have value; traffic that does not convert is charity (id=2033807753547043235). All-blog sites still have to answer “do you convert? I think you should do a money page” (id=2091775475635073373).
3. **Quality is a site-wide ranking factor. Its opposite is Spam. EEAT itself is not.** “EEAT is not a ranking factor (Google said). EEAT determines quality, and Google said Quality is a ranking factor” (id=1978391845844426780). Quality should consider the whole site (id=2092027807719444899). Quality’s definition is Effort and Original (id=2091362717907251353). Later four-part of high-quality content: First-hand experience + Originality + Non-commodity + Effort (handwritten, not AI) (id=2092977176501228007). Do not drop Effort + Original. Authority cannot offset AI Spam / AI Slope / junk blogs (id=2089165558516437461). Keep the March tension `[once]`: “It’s not about quality. It’s about how it was created” (id=2033808549445570669) — same human-input insistence; do not quote one without the other.
4. **The EEAT door is Effort + Originality. Generic automation cannot cross it. Tokens are not Effort.** (id=2092095823731462302) Your own process / agent / prompts are fine; do not sell a universal Agent (id=2092073561171398727). Any batch work needs increment (original text / images / video / insights) or it is judged low effort (id=2092090944648581505). Semi-auto beats full-auto, especially on Google (id=2033806758175772940). Scale + Quality + Speed cannot all be had without spending money `[once]` (id=2090567084933787767). PSEO without an original spine gets smashed within half a year (id=2088639745807278589).

### C. Technical foundation

On a decline, this block moves to the front. Technical SEO “is all the Foundation” (id=2091301352463102268).

1. **`robots.txt`** — did it ban crawlers
2. **Crawl status**
3. **Soft 404:** error pages must return HTTP 404, not 200. A 302 to a 200 error page is still Soft 404 (id=2091349098553295075)
4. **Formula:** `Raw = Rendered`; `Desktop = Mobile`; one HTML appears once; no `display:none` second copy; no separate m-site (Canonical does not save it); domestic H5 / mini-program templates default to unfit (id=2091508567119642847) (id=2091008050262110227). “The site must do SSR” `[once]` (id=2091699035929518242)
5. **Tool:** Screaming Frog is the one must-have (“other tools optional”). Raw vs rendered. Lumar + Botify = enterprise Frog; do not recommend installing them at mid-size (colleagues will hate you) (id=2092784685650997342) (id=2092723929249132561)
6. **CWV field data:** https://cruxvis.withgoogle.com/#/ — origin + all, 28d, LCP / CLS / INP. Lighthouse / PSI = lab / debug only. CWV is **not** a named ranking factor; with other page-experience it is ranking-related. Don’t over-invest; don’t lag competitors; late-game all-green helps; do it early because migration is painful (id=2092889660783722868)
7. **Mobile first screen:** content present; H1 on the first screen; overlays not covering the fold. GSC / Rich Results Test (id=2090686787534598639)
8. **Semantic HTML:** `<main>` = MC; do not div-to-the-bottom (id=2090738619808088255). Ecommerce: product cards in `<article>`, breadcrumbs, complete schema (id=2092036772687716771). Sitemap `changefreq` / `priority` can be deleted (id=2039659702192427443)
9. **Login page** (payment / membership): disclosure, firewall, register / recover, not a too-thin third-party login (id=2091376367740789153)
10. **Migration:** URL versions controlled; 301 one-to-one; SEO Review before the change (id=2091309003397902703). He only dropped the official doc — go there; do not invent steps: https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9370220
11. **JS-heavy / fancy effects:** in Chrome, turn JavaScript off. Look at https://www.apple.com/iphone/. Many effects can be written in CSS — that is the most SEO-friendly write. If something important disappears on your site, fix that. Not every crawler can process JS; core content hidden under JS may be invisible. Crawlers he named (not a capability table): Google, Bing, OpenAI, Claude, Grok, Meta, DuckDuckGo, Common Crawl, Deepseek, Doubao, Bytedance, Baidu, Yahoo, Naver. (id=2092105285955473656)

Once tech is level, the fight is the page’s final form: every tag, word, alt, anchor vs the competitor.
